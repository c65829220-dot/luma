About Luma

More Than Just Coffee

A small café built around a simple idea: slow down, and let the coffee do the talking.

Our Story

It started with one small roaster and a big idea.

Luma Coffee opened its doors in Gulberg with a simple goal — to bring specialty-grade coffee to a neighborhood that deserved better than instant mixes and rushed orders. What began as a single espresso machine and a handful of regulars has grown into a full café, but the heart of it hasn't changed: good coffee, made with care, served without pretense.

Today, Luma is where students plan projects, freelancers meet deadlines, and old friends catch up over a cup that actually tastes like something.

Our Philosophy

Slow down. Everything tastes better that way.

We believe a café should be a pause button, not another errand. That means unhurried service, comfortable seating, and a menu built on things worth waiting for — not shortcuts.

It also means treating every detail — the water temperature, the grind size, the last five minutes in the oven — as if it's the only thing that matters. Because to the cup in front of you, it is.
